# Flick Plan

## Transcript
1. Your website is your first impression. Most businesses waste it.
2. Slow pages. Broken layouts. Visitors leave in three seconds.
3. Orbit Web Designs builds fast, modern websites that turn visitors into customers.
4. Design, speed, SEO - done right, from Nairobi to the world.
5. Orbit Web Designs. Your brand, in motion. Visit orbitwebdesigns.co.ke today.

## Format
9:16 vertical, 1080×1920. Total runtime ~22s.

## Brand assets
None supplied. Flick will render an original "ORBIT" wordmark with an orbital-ring motif as the stand-in brand mark.

## Creative direction
User: "you do you." Interpretation: dark cosmic theme fitting the Orbit name — deep space navy background, warm amber/orange accent, crisp white type, smooth confident motion. Problem scenes feel cold and broken; solution scenes feel fast and polished.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — wasted-first-impression — 0:00–0:04

Transcript:
"Your website is your first impression. Most businesses waste it."

What's on screen:
A browser window loads a dull gray generic business site. A loading spinner spins too long, then the page renders flat and dated. A visitor cursor arrives, hesitates, and bounces away. The whole window dims to near-black as the line lands.

Text on screen:
"Your first impression" appears over the browser as it loads.

Brand assets / supplied material:
None — recreated generic site is intentional.

Sequential / interaction:
Browser chrome appears → progress bar stalls → page renders → cursor moves in, hovers, exits top-right → scene dims.

Sound effect:
Click.mp3 when the page finally renders; Impact.mp3 when the cursor bounces away.

Audio-coupled idea:
The dim-to-black hits exactly on the Impact sound.

Transition:
Hard cut.

### Scene 2 — three-second-exit — 0:04–0:08

Transcript:
"Slow pages. Broken layouts. Visitors leave in three seconds."

What's on screen:
The same site breaks apart: text blocks slide out of alignment, an image overlaps a heading, buttons misplace. A large countdown timer counts down from 3 while a visitor count ticks away. At zero the screen empties.

Text on screen:
"3 seconds" beside the countdown digits.

Brand assets / supplied material:
None.

Sequential / interaction:
Three breakages land one by one (text shift, overlap, misplaced button) synced to the countdown digits 3 → 2 → 1; at 0 all elements slide off-screen leaving empty dark canvas.

Sound effect:
Popups.mp3 on each of the three breakages; Notification.mp3 at zero.

Audio-coupled idea:
Each breakage lands exactly on its countdown digit.

Transition:
Zoom out into darkness.

### Scene 3 — orbit-builds-better — 0:08–0:13

Transcript:
"Orbit Web Designs builds fast, modern websites that turn visitors into customers."

What's on screen:
A fresh browser window snaps together piece by piece: nav bar, hero headline, two feature cards, CTA button — each element arriving cleanly. A speed badge stamps "98" performance. A visitor cursor glides to the CTA and clicks; the button pulses and a customer card with a green check slides up.

Text on screen:
Hero headline "Websites that work."; button label "Get started"; badge "98".

Brand assets / supplied material:
None — original ORBIT-styled mockup.

Sequential / interaction:
Five UI pieces snap in one by one → performance badge stamps → cursor travels → click → conversion card reveals.

Sound effect:
Pop.mp3 per snapping piece; Zoomin-OR-out.mp3 on the badge stamp; Click.mp3 on the CTA click; Correct.mp3 when the check appears.

Audio-coupled idea:
Every snap, stamp, click, and check lands on its own sound.

Transition:
Slide left.

### Scene 4 — design-speed-seo — 0:13–0:17

Transcript:
"Design, speed, SEO - done right, from Nairobi to the world."

What's on screen:
Three amber tiles rise from below one at a time: DESIGN (type specimens fan out), SPEED (gauge needle sweeps high), SEO (a rank chip climbs #8 → #1). Below them a small globe pulse expands outward from Nairobi, rings reaching the frame edges.

Text on screen:
Tile labels "DESIGN", "SPEED", "SEO"; rank chips "8", "1"; location tag "Nairobi".

Brand assets / supplied material:
None.

Sequential / interaction:
Tiles arrive in order, each animating its own proof; globe pulse fires after the third tile settles.

Sound effect:
Pop.mp3 per tile arrival; energy.MP3 on the globe pulse.

Audio-coupled idea:
Globe pulse expands exactly on the energy sound.

Transition:
Crossfade.

### Scene 5 — orbit-outro — 0:17–0:22

Transcript:
"Orbit Web Designs. Your brand, in motion. Visit orbitwebdesigns.co.ke today."

What's on screen:
Dark starfield. Three glowing tiles labeled DESIGN, SPEED, SEO orbit a central bright point, spiral inward, and merge into the ORBIT wordmark wrapped by an orbital ring. Beneath it the URL types itself out, ending on a settled hold.

Text on screen:
"ORBIT WEB DESIGNS"; tagline "Your brand, in motion."; URL "orbitwebdesigns.co.ke".

Brand assets / supplied material:
None — original ORBIT wordmark rendered by Flick.

Sequential / interaction:
Tiles orbit and merge → ring draws around the wordmark → URL types character by character → full lockup holds.

Sound effect:
riser.mp3 under the merge; Impact.mp3 when the ring completes around the wordmark; Typing.mp3 during the URL type-out.

Audio-coupled idea:
Ring completion lands exactly on the Impact; each URL character appears on typing rhythm.

Transition:
End on held final frame.
