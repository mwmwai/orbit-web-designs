# Flick Plan — Orbit Web Designs 9:16

**Aspect:** 9:16 (1080x1920) · **Brand assets:** logo-mark.png, logo.png (electric #00c8ff / neon #2f7bff on ink #0a0a0f) · **Audio:** sound effects only, no music

## Scene 1 — Lost in Silence
- **Transcript:** "Your website loses you customers before they even read a word." (0–4s)
- **On screen:** Dark phone frame center, grey wireframe site with pulsing loading bar. Silhouettes walk toward phone then U-turn away. Big serif text behind: "LOST"
- **Text on screen:** "Loses customers" (electric)
- **Assets:** logo-mark tiny watermark bottom
- **Interaction:** Sequential — loader stalls → silhouettes bounce
- **Sound:** `impact-soft` on bounce + `typing` for text reveal
- **Transition:** quick streak wipe

## Scene 2 — 3 Second Rule
- **Transcript:** "Visitors decide in 3 seconds. If yours is slow or outdated, they're already gone." (4–8s)
- **On screen:** Giant countdown 3 → 2 → 1 behind phone. Phone shows outdated site (serif clutter, no M-Pesa). At 0, phone cracks dark and visitors fade.
- **Text on screen:** "3 seconds" huge, then "GONE"
- **Assets:** none extra
- **Interaction:** Countdown ticks sync to words
- **Sound:** `counter` tick each second, `whoosh` at gone
- **Transition:** orbit ring spin

## Scene 3 — Orbit Builds Fast
- **Transcript:** "We build fast, modern websites that turn visitors into paying customers." (8–13s)
- **On screen:** Phone flips to Orbit modern site (clean hero, M-Pesa badge, 95 Speed badge). Electric orbit rings spin behind (same as Hero3D). Visitors now tap → KES badge pings + M-Pesa Till feed scrolls.
- **Text on screen:** "Fast · Modern · Paid"
- **Assets:** logo.png bottom, M-Pesa tag
- **Interaction:** Phone flip simulated, tap → pay animation
- **Sound:** `click` on flip, `counter` on KES pop, `reveal` on speed badge
- **Transition:** glow expand

## Scene 4 — Trusted Across Nairobi
- **Transcript:** "Trusted by businesses across Nairobi and beyond." (13–17s)
- **On screen:** Dark map of Kenya → zoom to Nairobi pins popping (salon, shop, law firm). Small testimonial chips float: "Page 1 in 3 months" · "100s M-Pesa/mo".
- **Text on screen:** "50+ sites · 25+ M-Pesa stores"
- **Assets:** logo-mark as map pin
- **Interaction:** Sequential pin pops around map
- **Sound:** `pop` per pin (3x)
- **Transition:** map fade to ink

## Scene 5 — Your Brand in Motion

- **On screen:** Center ORBIT wordmark spaced 0.3em (as Navbar) + logo-mark orbiting (like Hero rings). URL types below with cursor. Electric→neon gradient line draws under.
- **Text on screen:** "ORBIT / WEB DESIGNS & MARKETING" + "orbitwebdesigns.co.ke"
