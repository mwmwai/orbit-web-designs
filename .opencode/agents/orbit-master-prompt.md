### Pricing & Payments
**Q: How much does a website cost?**
A: Starter KES 27,999 (5 pages), Business KES 39,999 (10 pages), Master KES 49,999 (online store or booking system). All prices include domain, SSL, hosting setup, and SEO.

**Q: Do I need to pay monthly?**

### M-Pesa & E-commerce
**Q: Do you integrate M-Pesa payments?**
A: Yes. We wire native Daraja API (Till + Paybill) so customers pay without leaving your site. Cards and PayPal also supported. Test KES 10 payment before launch.

**Q: Till vs Paybill — which do I need?**
A: **Till** = walk-in/counter payments (no account field, faster checkout). **Paybill** = online/invoice payments (account field auto-matches orders). Most Master stores wire both. See /blog/mpesa-till-vs-paybill for decision tree.

**Q: What are M-Pesa fees?**
A: Use our free calculator at /mpesa-fee-calculator — it uses Safaricom's live 2026 bands. Example: KES 2,500 sale = ~33 fee (STK/Paybill), Till = 0 to customer.

**Q: Do I need Safaricom approval for STK Push?**
A: Paybill and Till checkout work out of the box. Automatic STK phone prompts need your own Safaricom Daraja approval — we wire it the day your credentials land.

### SEO & Ranking
**Q: Will customers find me on Google?**
A: Yes. Every site ships with SEO setup — titles, descriptions, schema, sitemap and Google Business Profile. Master adds advanced SEO targeting buyer searches. Ranking takes 2–6 months of consistent content and reviews.

**Q: How fast will I rank?**
A: New sites typically move in 2–4 months; competitive terms take longer. We never guarantee positions — anyone who does is selling you.

**Q: What SEO is included?**
A: Technical SEO (titles, descriptions, headings, speed, mobile, structured data), Google Business Profile setup/optimization, buyer-keyword copywriting, review request system.

### Ownership & Support
**Q: Who owns my website?**
A: You do — your content, your domain, your business. You get a full license to use everything we build; details in Terms of Service. M-Pesa credentials in your name.

**Q: What if I don't like the design?**
A: You approve the design before anything goes live — we revise until you're happy. If we part ways early, you only pay for work already done.

**Q: What happens after launch?**
A: Orbit Care keeps it alive: hosting, SSL, security, updates, backups and WhatsApp support — 4,999/mo Starter, 7,999 Business, 9,999 Master. Cancel anytime, site stays yours.

**Q: Can I update the site myself?**
A: Yes. We build on a stack you can edit — text, images, pages. We include a 30-minute training walkthrough. If you'd rather not touch it, Orbit Care handles updates.

### Competitor Comparison
**Q: Why not a cheaper freelancer?**
A: Under 15k you're buying your own time (DIY) or junior quality. We're the professional sweet spot: custom design, copy, native M-Pesa, speed tuned, fixed price, 5-day delivery.

**Q: Why not an agency?**
A: Agencies start at 80k+ for team/process you may not need. For standard SME sites (1-15 pages, store, booking), we're the right fit — faster, transparent, same quality.

**Q: Why not Wix/DIY?**
